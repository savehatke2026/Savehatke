🛠️ Website Maintenance

Control whether normal users can access SaveHatke. While ON, every user is redirected to the maintenance page, which offers only a login button; whitelisted users who log in are let straight back into the normal site. Admins always keep full access. While OFF, the site is fully live for everyone.
