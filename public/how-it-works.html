The Journey

From discovery to delivery

Here's exactly what happens after you click "buy" — no magic, no surprises.

1
Discovery

You find a coupon you want

You browse the marketplace, see a verified SaveFlat 500 OFF coupon for your favourite brand, and decide you want it. The face value is ₹500, you see it listed at, say, ₹40 — that's the price you pay.

2
Checkout

Secure payment via Razorpay

You click Buy and are taken to a Razorpay checkout. Pay with UPI, debit card, credit card, net-banking, or any wallet. SaveHatke never sees your card details — Razorpay is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified.

3
Delivery

Code lands in your dashboard & inbox

The moment payment is confirmed, the coupon code is delivered to your SaveHatke dashboard under "My Coupons" and emailed to you. The code is locked to you — it can't be sold again.

4
Use

Paste at the brand's checkout

Copy the code from your dashboard, head to the brand's site or app, paste it in the promo-code field at checkout, and watch the discount apply. The coupon's expiry date is shown on the card so you know how long you have.

5
Settlement

Seller gets paid, marketplace stays alive

Behind the scenes, the platform fee is deducted from the sale, the seller's share (typically 70–80% of the sale price) is credited to their SaveHatke wallet, and the original coupon code is permanently retired. Everyone wins.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The most common things people ask before getting started. Don't see yours? Drop us a line.

Is SaveHatke free to join? +
Yes — creating an account is completely free. You only pay when you buy a coupon, or earn when you sell one. There are no subscription fees, no listing fees, and no hidden charges.
How do I get paid when I sell a coupon? +
Earnings from each sale land in your SaveHatke wallet instantly. You can withdraw to any Indian bank account or UPI ID from your wallet page — no minimum amount, no waiting period. Most withdrawals settle in minutes via UPI.
What if a coupon code doesn't work? +
This is extremely rare (every code is reviewed before listing) but if it happens, open a support ticket from the support page and we'll investigate. If the code is invalid, you get a full refund — no questions asked.
Can I sell coupons that are about to expire? +
Yes, but buyers will price them accordingly. We show the expiry date clearly on every coupon card, so you know exactly how much time the buyer has to use it. Coupons with very short expiry windows can still sell — often at a steeper discount.
Is my payment information safe? +
Yes. All payments run through Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified (the same security standard used by major banks). SaveHatke never sees or stores your card details — they go directly to Razorpay's secure servers.
How long does it take for my coupon to be listed? +
Most submissions are reviewed within 2–6 hours during business hours. If you submit a screenshot, the review is even faster. You'll get an email the moment your coupon goes live on the marketplace.
What if I change my mind about a purchase? +
Because the coupon code is delivered the moment payment is confirmed, we can't offer change-of-mind refunds once the code has been revealed. If the code itself turns out to be invalid (very rare), you're covered by our refund guarantee.
