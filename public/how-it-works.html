How SaveHatke Works

Buy coupons you can use. Sell coupons you don't need.

Two ways to use SaveHatke

Buy a coupon

Find a discount you can actually use.

Browse available coupons, check the important details such as brand, discount, price, and validity, then purchase a coupon that suits you.

  1. 01

    Browse

    Explore available coupons in the marketplace.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Open a listing and check its details, including discount, validity, and price.

  3. 03

    Purchase

    Complete the purchase using the available payment process.

  4. 04

    Receive

    After the purchase is successfully completed, the coupon information becomes available through the appropriate SaveHatke flow and your dashboard.

  5. 05

    Use

    Use the coupon according to the brand's terms before it expires.

Sell a coupon

Turn an eligible unused coupon into value.

If you have an eligible coupon that you don't need, you can submit it through SaveHatke's seller flow. After the applicable review and approval process, it can be made available to buyers.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Provide the required coupon information through the Sell Coupons page.

  2. 02

    Review

    The submitted coupon can be reviewed according to SaveHatke's eligibility requirements.

  3. 03

    List

    If approved, the coupon can become available on the marketplace.

  4. 04

    Sell

    A buyer can purchase the listed coupon.

  5. 05

    Payout

    After the applicable transaction conditions are completed, the seller becomes eligible for the corresponding payout according to SaveHatke's process.

When you buy a coupon

  1. Choose

    You find a coupon you want.

  2. Review

    You check the available coupon details.

  3. Pay

    You complete the purchase.

  4. Receive

    The purchased coupon is made available to you through SaveHatke.

  5. Use

    You redeem it with the applicable brand according to its terms.

When you sell a coupon

  1. Submit

    You provide the required coupon details.

  2. Review

    SaveHatke checks the submission against its requirements.

  3. Approval

    Eligible coupons can be approved for marketplace listing.

  4. Purchase

    A buyer purchases the coupon.

  5. Payout

    The seller receives the applicable payout after the required conditions are completed.

Before you buy or sell

For buyers

  • Check the coupon's brand and discount.
  • Check the validity and expiry information.
  • Review the listing details before purchasing.
  • Make sure the coupon is suitable for your intended purchase.

For sellers

  • Only submit coupons that meet SaveHatke's eligibility requirements.
  • Provide accurate coupon information.
  • Make sure the submitted information is correct.
  • Follow the seller flow and review process.
