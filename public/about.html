Our story

Why SaveHatke exists

People receive promotional codes, vouchers and discount coupons that they may never use. Most of them sit in an inbox or an app until the date passes and the value disappears with it.

Instead of allowing eligible coupons to remain unused until they expire, SaveHatke creates a marketplace where sellers can offer them to buyers who may actually need them. Nothing about the coupon changes — only whether anyone gets to use it.
