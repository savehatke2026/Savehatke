01 Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate our coupon marketplace, process transactions, maintain accounts, provide support, and protect the Platform.

1.1 Information You Provide

Account Information — When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account authentication information

Coupon Information — When you sell a coupon, we may collect:

  • Coupon code
  • Brand name
  • Coupon category
  • Coupon value / details
  • Selling price
  • Expiry date
  • Coupon description
  • Other information necessary to verify and list the coupon

When you buy a coupon, we may collect information relating to:

  • Purchased coupon
  • Order details
  • Transaction details
  • Purchase status

Payment Information — When you make a payment, payment processing may be handled by Razorpay or another authorized payment service.

We may receive transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction ID
  • Payment status
  • Order / reference ID
  • Payment-related details required to confirm the transaction

We do not intend to store your complete card, banking, or other sensitive payment credentials on SaveHatke's own systems.

Seller Payout Information — To process eligible seller payouts, we may collect your:

  • UPI ID
  • Payout-related transaction / reference information

Reviews & Ratings — If you submit a review or rating after a completed transaction, we may collect:

  • Rating
  • Review / comment
  • Related transaction information
  • Information necessary to display and moderate the review

Support Information — If you contact us for assistance, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Subject
  • Message and other information you voluntarily provide

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

We may collect limited technical information necessary for the operation and security of the Platform, such as:

  • Pages or sections accessed
  • Features used
  • Session information
  • Date and time of activity
  • Basic browser or device information necessary for website functionality

We do not intentionally collect browsing history outside SaveHatke for advertising or unrelated tracking purposes.

08 Cookies & Local Storage

We group cookies into three categories. Essential cookies are always on because the Platform cannot function without them. Analytics and Marketing cookies stay switched off until you choose to allow them, and the scripts behind them are not downloaded before that.

You can review or change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the footer of any page. Your choice is stored in a cookie on your device, so it is remembered when you come back, and it applies to that browser only — a different browser, a different device, or a private window will ask again.

Essential cookies — always active

Used for signing in, keeping your session secure, protecting our forms against automated abuse, and remembering your cookie choice.

  • sh_session — SaveHatke · 48 hours · your signed-in session. Contains a random session identifier only.
  • sh_consent — SaveHatke · 180 days · the cookie choice you make on this site.
  • __cf_bm, cf_chl_* — Cloudflare Turnstile · up to 30 minutes · bot and abuse protection on the login and support forms.
  • Razorpay checkout cookies — Razorpay · session · set only while a payment is in progress, so that payment can complete securely.

Analytics cookies — off until you allow them

Aggregated, statistical measurement: how many people visited a page, which coupons get viewed, where people stop. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Marketing cookies — off until you allow them

Used to make the coupon offers you see more relevant and to measure whether our campaigns worked.

🍪 No analytics or marketing cookies are in use today. SaveHatke currently runs no analytics, advertising or retargeting provider at all, so those two categories are empty in practice. The consent controls are live and enforced regardless: if we ever add one, it cannot load or set a cookie until you have agreed, and this section will name it, its provider and how long it lasts.

We never store passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive personal data in a cookie.

🚫 No Unnecessary Tracking. SaveHatke does not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or unrelated browsing-history tracking. We do not use your SaveHatke activity to build an advertising profile for sale to third parties.
